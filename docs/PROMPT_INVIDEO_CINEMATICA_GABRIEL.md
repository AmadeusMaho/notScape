# Prompt InVideo · Cinemática «La caída de Gabriel» (tutorial · Acto 5→6)

Uso: pegar el bloque «PROMPT» en InVideo (modo texto a vídeo). Canon: `docs/GUION_TUTORIAL.md` (DECISIONES v2) y `docs/LORE.md` §1b. **Es referencia de dirección de arte/guion, NO sustituye a la cinemática del juego** (esa se hará en three.js, por código, en la Fase del tutorial). No revela canon: Gabriel dice solo la frase acordada y el fragmento NO se explica.

## PROMPT (copiar desde aquí)

Create a 45-second cinematic, 16:9, 24 fps, dark fantasy painterly style (oil-painting look, "No Rest for the Wicked" meets "Dark Souls" — muted desaturated shadows, a single warm saturated accent: ember orange and gold). Slow, heavy camera. NO text on screen except one final title card. No modern elements.

Setting: a ruined floating island among clouds, high in the sky. Broken white-stone temple arches, shattered angel statues with erased faces, a sea of clouds below, low sun. Dusk.

Characters:
- GABRIEL: a tall fallen angel, once majestic. Tattered white-and-grey robes, cracked golden halo, huge wings with burnt, missing feathers, a face weary and sorrowful, not monstrous. Kneeling, wounded.
- THE PLAYER: a lone hooded wanderer seen mostly from behind, a worn blade in hand. Never show the face.

Shot list:
1. (0–6s) Wide shot. Gabriel kneels at the centre of the broken temple, one wing dragging on the stone. Loose feathers drift. The wanderer stands in the foreground, silhouetted, breathing hard. Wind and distant low choir hum.
2. (6–12s) Close-up on Gabriel's eyes, dim gold. He lifts his head, almost relieved. His last strike was slow and long; the sword has already landed. A single feather falls past the lens.
3. (12–20s) Medium shot, low angle. Gabriel whispers (voice-over, hoarse, calm, subtitle-free): "Ahora también estarás maldito. Ve a liberar el mundo." On the last word his halo cracks and goes dark. His body begins to dissolve into golden dust and ash-white feathers.
4. (20–28s) THE TIME FRAGMENT: from Gabriel's chest a small crystalline shard of pale gold and deep amber rises, slowly spinning, pulsing like a heartbeat. It floats to the wanderer's outstretched hand. As it approaches, TIME STOPS: feathers, dust, wind-blown cloth and clouds freeze mid-air; sound fades to a single high held tone; the only motion is the shard's slow pulse and a faint ripple of light. Extreme close-up of frozen particles suspended around the hand. (Do not explain what the shard is.)
5. (28–34s) The shard sinks into the wanderer's palm, leaving a faint glowing mark. Time snaps back with a deep low thud. The sound returns all at once.
6. (34–42s) THE SKY BURNS: the horizon cracks open in orange and crimson, fire spreads across the cloud sea as if the heavens themselves ignite. Burning feathers rain down. The island trembles, stone arches crumble, fragments tear away and fall into the burning clouds. Wide epic shot: the wanderer small against a sky of fire, the shard's light the only calm gold in the scene. Music swells: choir, low strings, distant bells, a deep brass drone.
7. (42–45s) Hard cut to black. Single title card, thin serif gold lettering, centred: "Cien años." Silence.

Mood: solemn, tragic, awe, not horror. Gabriel is dignified, a guardian at the end of an age. Camera: slow push-ins, no handheld shake except during the island collapse. Color grade: oil-painted, dark vignette, warm ember glow as the only saturated accent. Audio: orchestral dark fantasy score, wind, one whispered line, silence during the time stop.

Negative / avoid: no gore, no blood, no demon look for Gabriel, no visible face for the wanderer, no on-screen UI, no cartoon or anime style, no neon, no modern clothing, no extra dialogue, no text other than "Cien años."

## Variantes útiles
- **Versión corta (20 s):** plano 3 (frase) + plano 4 (tiempo detenido) + plano 6 (cielo arde) + tarjeta final.
- **Voz en español:** pedir a InVideo voz masculina grave, susurrada, en español; la frase exacta es «Ahora también estarás maldito. Ve a liberar el mundo.»
- **Para ajustar:** si InVideo muestra el rostro del jugador, repetir el prompt añadiendo "the wanderer is always shot from behind or in silhouette".

## Cuidados de lore (no romper)
- El fragmento del TIEMPO no se nombra ni se explica en la cinemática.
- «Ahora también» queda ambiguo (¿exilio? ¿Nul'ru?): no añadir diálogo ni flashbacks.
- Ru NO aparece (decisión pendiente ★ en el guion); la tarjeta «Cien años.» enlaza con el Acto 7.
- La isla es la misma que será el bioma Cielo del endgame (Fase 53).
